# Materials estimate

The estimate sums the precious-metal content of the fitted casting and estimated loose-stone costs in USD. The number updates as the design changes and rounds to the nearest $100. Positive totals below $50 display as “< $100”. A missing price for any selected stone leaves the total blank. Opening the estimate shows which component lacks a comparable. Labor, casting loss, alloying additions, tax, shipping and retail ring markup are outside this calculation.

## Metal

The viewer integrates the signed volume of the closed metal triangles after fitting, converts cubic millimetres to cubic centimetres, and multiplies by the alloy density in the library. The resulting grams include the band, gallery, claws and shoulders. The fine gold, silver, platinum or palladium fraction is multiplied by its reference price. The November 17, 2026 snapshot uses $123.86/g gold, $1.52/g silver, $45.78/g platinum and $30.67/g palladium. [Kitco precious metals](https://www.kitco.com/price/precious-metals)

## Gemstones

The center uses the selected carat weight. Each accent uses its diamond-equivalent native weight multiplied by the ratio of the selected stone's density to diamond density. Individual stone overrides are included. The rates are reference estimates for attractive commercial material. Origin, treatment, cutting, clarity and dealer margins can change actual asking prices substantially.

- Natural and laboratory-grown diamonds have separate size curves from the November 16, 2026 StoneAlgo listing indices. Common natural cuts have shape adjustments where the index publishes comparable numbers. Other cuts use the mixed-shape index. Small accents use melee price references, interpolated into the center-size curve. [Natural diamond index](https://www.stonealgo.com/diamond-prices/) · [Lab-grown index](https://www.stonealgo.com/lab-grown-diamond-prices/) · [Natural melee](https://www.gurudiam.com/natural-diamonds/melee) · [Lab-grown melee](https://caraturagems.com/pages/lab-grown-diamond-melee-price-per-carat)
- Colored stone rates use representative values within published dealer ranges. For varieties with two reference rates, the viewer interpolates dollars per carat between 1 and 2.5 ct and uses the endpoint rate beyond that interval. These represent approximate comparisons, including when applied to small colored accents.
